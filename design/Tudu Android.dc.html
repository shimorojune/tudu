Two permissions,
then you are set up. Both are about one thing: a reminder arriving at the minute you asked for, even after the phone has been idle all night.
1
Notifications Reminders, requests from your co-workers, and the silent summary of your day. Granted
2
Alarms & reminders Without this Android is free to batch your 10:30 reminder into the next time it happens to wake up. Often twenty minutes late. Allow exact alarms
3
Connect JIRA Optional, and skippable forever. Tudu is a working to-do app with no JIRA at all.
