Motion in the Notebook

What moves, how fast and with which curve. Every value is a Tudu Paper token. With Reduce motion on, all of it happens at once.

Opening the note window

The note window fades in with a 6px lift and a hair of scale, the cursor already in a blank note. It never brings the Tudu window forward. Closing reverses it.

in 220ms ease-arrive · out 180ms ease-change

Fill the screen

The arrows (or a double-click on the bar) ease the window out to the screen’s edges, keeping a small margin; the corners soften a touch. Again eases it back to where it was.

frame dur-panel-in ease-settle

Drag and resize

The window follows the pointer exactly while you drag the bar or the corner, with no easing; it only eases when it snaps back inside the screen.

1:1 while dragging · settle dur-panel-in

Pin

The pin tilts and inks in; the window’s edge firms up a little so a pinned note is recognisable. A toast says what pinning does the first time only.

tilt dur-menu · edge dur-panel-in

Recent notes

The rail folds to nothing from its width while its contents fade; the note takes the room. Picking a note turns the note in place (a short fade and 4px lift); the rail never moves.

rail dur-panel-in ease-settle · turn dur-arrive

Folders

The chevron turns a quarter; the children open by easing their own height, so the rows under them slide down smoothly instead of jumping. Closing is the same move back.

chevron dur-menu · height dur-fold ease-change

Saving

Typing shows nothing. If a save takes more than half a second, a small ring turns where the tick will be; when it lands, a tick draws itself in and fades away. Only a problem stays: “Not saved yet”, “Didn’t save” or “Changed elsewhere”, in amber, until it’s sorted.

ring after 500ms · tick draw dur-tick, gone at 1.4s

Link a task

The picker drops 4px under its button. A picked task pops in as a chip (94% to full, with a fade) while the chips after it and the Link button glide to their new places and the row eases to its new height. Unlinking shrinks the chip away first, then the rest close the gap the same way.

pop dur-menu · chip dur-arrive · glide 240ms ease-settle · out 170ms

Notes in a task

New note opens the note window (it fades in with a 6px lift) on a blank note already linked to the task. Its row slides into the list as the window opens; the rows below glide down. Unlinking folds the row away to the right and the rest close up.

row dur-row-open · out 200ms · glide 240ms

Open a note from Search

Search closes as usual and the Notebook page opens on that note, its folder already open and the row filled in.

search out 150ms · page at once

Banners

Offline and “Changed in another window” open their own room above the note: the space grows from 0 as the banner fades in, so the words never jump.

grow + fade 280ms ease-arrive

Make a task from words

Select words in a note and a small “Make a task” button rises 4px above them as it fades in. It follows the selection, and goes when the selection does.

rise dur-menu ease-arrive

Phones

A note turns in from the right like a day; ‹ Notebook turns back from the left. The keyboard rises over the editor, which scrolls to keep the cursor in view.

turn dur-day-turn ease-arrive
